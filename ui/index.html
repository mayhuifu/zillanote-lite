    Remembered voices

    Name a speaker once, and ZillaNote knows the voice in later meetings and names it by itself.

    1. Open a meeting and choose its Transcript tab.
    2. Above the transcript, click a speaker, such as Speaker 1 · 04:12
    3. Type the name and press Enter. To use the name in the minutes too, press Rewrite minutes under the meeting.

      A voice named wrongly can be renamed the same way, or forgotten here. Voices are told apart on this computer by pyannote's segmentation-3.0 (MIT) and WeSpeaker's ResNet34 embedding as packaged by pyannote (CC BY 4.0).
